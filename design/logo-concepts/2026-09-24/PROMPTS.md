# ORION Clinic — logo concept prompts

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool, one independent generation per concept. Original raster PNG outputs are preserved unchanged. No application asset was replaced. These are visual concepts, not final vector master artwork or trademark clearance.

## 01-orbit

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: polished original identity concept for ORION Clinic, a clinical care platform, including future mobile application.
Style: exceptional Swiss editorial brand design, flat vector-like geometry in a raster image, confidently minimal, precise optical balance and generous negative space, human healthcare meets calm technology. Original memorable mark, no resemblance to a known company.
Presentation: square identity board. Upper approximately 70% is warm ivory #F1F3F0, featuring one large centered horizontal logo lockup: symbol on left, exact wordmark ORION with small Clinic below on right. Lower 30% is solid dark forest #161B1A, featuring a smaller identical lockup in mint #82C8BA and ivory. This is one concept in two colorways, not multiple alternative symbols.
Typography: bold geometric humanist sans serif similar to Golos Text, modest letter spacing for uppercase ORION, precisely spelled ORION (O R I O N), smaller mixed-case Clinic. Only those words. Teal #125F56 and dark #17211F in light version, mint #82C8BA in dark version.
Constraints: crisp broad geometry, readable symbol at 32 px, 1–2 solid ink colors, no gradients, no glow, no bevel, no 3D, no photography, no presentation mockups, no decorative background patterns, no tagline, no labels, no watermark, no medical red cross, no fine lines.
Concept: Create an elegant distinctive ORBIT O: a bold near-circular O crossed by a single confidently broad asymmetrical elliptical sweep with one integrated offset small solid circular node. The movement evokes the constellation Orion, a continuous patient journey and coordinated care. The logo is one coherent compact silhouette, not a scientific atom or a planet illustration. Fine optical balance, curved geometry, timeless technology brand.
